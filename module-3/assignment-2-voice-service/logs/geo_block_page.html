Is ElevenLabs available in all countries and regions?

Access to ElevenLabs depends on applicable sanctions and regional service availability.

We restrict access from the following countries and regions:

  • Belarus
  • Cuba
  • Iran
  • North Korea
  • Russia
  • Syria
  • Crimea, and the Donetsk and Luhansk oblasts of Ukraine

Access may also be unavailable in other regions where we do not currently support the service.

These restrictions can sometimes affect connections from outside the locations listed above if our cloud provider associates an IP address with a restricted location.

If you believe you have been incorrectly blocked, please contact support@elevenlabs.io.
